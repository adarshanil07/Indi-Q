# Privacy Policy for Indi-Q

> This policy is published at
> **https://adarshanil07.github.io/Indiq.privacypolicy/privacy.html** —
> use that URL in store listings. This file is the source copy.

**Last updated: 26 July 2026**

Indi-Q ("the app", "we", "us") is a party word-guessing game published by
EnJoy Games. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the
app, and what choices you have.

We have tried to keep this short and specific, because the honest summary is
that the game itself collects very little.

---

## 1. We do not ask you for personal information

Indi-Q has no accounts, no sign-in, and no profiles. We never ask for your name,
email address, phone number, or location, and there is no server that receives
your gameplay.

Team names you type during setup are stored **only on your device** and are
never transmitted to us or anyone else.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The app saves two things locally so the game behaves sensibly between sessions:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your last game setup (team names, timer length, and similar options) | So you do not have to re-enter them every time |
| Your card language preference (English or Malayalam) | So the app opens in the language you chose |

This information stays on your device, is not readable by us, and is deleted
when you uninstall the app.

## 3. Advertising

Indi-Q is free and is funded by advertising. Ads are provided by **Google
AdMob**.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

- your device's **advertising identifier**
- device and general technical information (device model, operating system,
  language, coarse region)
- how you interact with the ads shown (for example, that an ad was displayed or
  tapped)

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us. We never receive this
information in a form that identifies you — we only see aggregate figures such
as how many ads were shown.

Google's handling of this data is described in their own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 4. Consent, and your choices

**If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland**,
we ask for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent
message. You may choose whether your data is used for personalised advertising.

You can **change or withdraw that choice at any time** from
**Settings → Ad privacy choices** inside the app. Withdrawing consent is as
easy as giving it. If you decline personalised ads, you will still see ads, but
they will be non-personalised.

**On any device**, you can also limit ad tracking through your system settings:

- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → *Delete advertising ID* (or *Opt out of
  Ads Personalisation*)
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

## 5. Legal bases (UK/EU GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on:

- **Consent** — for personalised advertising and any associated identifiers.
  You may withdraw this at any time, as described above.
- **Legitimate interests** — for showing non-personalised advertising in order
  to fund a free app, and for keeping the app functioning correctly and secure.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your
personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local
data protection authority. Because we do not hold identifiable information about
you ourselves, most such requests are best directed to Google using the links in
section 3. You are welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

## 6. Children

Indi-Q is intended for a **general audience** and is not directed at children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you
believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please
contact us and we will act on it.

## 7. Data retention

We do not operate servers and do not retain your personal data. Information
stored on your device remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own
retention policies.

## 8. Security

Because the app keeps your information on your own device and does not transmit
it to us, the main protection is your device itself. We recommend keeping your
operating system up to date and using a screen lock.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy — for example if we add a feature that changes what
data is involved. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the
current version, and material changes will be highlighted in the app or its
store listing.
